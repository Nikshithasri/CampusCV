#📄 Campus CV – ATS Resume Analyzer & Builder

Campus CV is a simple and powerful web application designed to help students and job seekers improve their resumes, beat Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), and apply for jobs with confidence.  
From analyzing resumes to building professional CVs and cover letters — everything is available in one place.

🚀 **Live Demo:** https://campus-cv.vercel.app/

---

## 🧠 What You Can Do with Campus CV

### 📝 ATS Resume Analyzer
- Upload your resume in **PDF, DOCX, or TXT** format  
- Paste a job description you’re applying for  
- Get insights on keyword matching, formatting, and ATS compatibility  
- Improve your resume to increase shortlisting chances  

### 🛠️ Resume Builder
- Build clean and professional resumes step by step  
- Easily add and edit sections like:
  - Work Experience  
  - Education  
  - Skills  
  - Projects  

### 📨 Cover Letter Generator
- Create customized cover letters for specific job roles  
- Save time by generating job-focused content instantly  

### 👤 User Profile
- Keep track of your resumes and applications  
- Save progress and reuse data whenever needed

## 🧪 How Campus CV Works

1. **Upload Your Resume**  
   Select or drag & drop your resume file.

2. **Add Job Description**  
   Paste the job posting you want to apply for.

3. **Analyze & Improve**  
   The system compares your resume with the job description and provides ATS-style feedback.

4. **Build & Download**  
   Create or update your resume and cover letter, then download them for applications.
